# Meetings

Prep before a meeting and notes after it, one meeting per pair of files. The `.html` page is the one you read; the `.md` file is your assistant's working copy. After the meeting, your notes are added to the same files.

## File names

- `YYYY-MM-DD - [Meeting Title].md` and `YYYY-MM-DD - [Meeting Title].html`, for example `2026-11-04 - Coffee with Maria Lopez.html`.
- Two meetings with similar titles on the same day get the start time too: `2026-11-04 0930 - [Meeting Title].html`.

Start from `Workflows/Templates/Meeting-Prep.md`.
