# People

One file per person or company your assistant should know: who they are, how you know them, what's open between you, and dated notes from meetings. Setup creates the first ones from the people you name; meeting prep and your notes add the rest.

## File names

- A person: `First Last.md`, for example `Maria Lopez.md`.
- A company or organization: `Name (company).md`, for example `Hill Country Bakery (company).md`.
- Not sure who it is yet: `Unknown - [short description].md`, renamed once you know.

One file per person. If someone shows up again, their existing file is updated, not copied. Start from `Workflows/Templates/Person.md` or `Workflows/Templates/Company.md`.
