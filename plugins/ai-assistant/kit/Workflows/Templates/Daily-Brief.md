# Daily Brief template

The daily brief is built from `Workflows/Templates/Daily-Brief.html`. Copy that file, fill it as its top comment explains, and save it as `Briefs/Daily-Brief.html` plus a dated copy. The steps are in `Workflows/Daily-Brief.md`.

Section order on the page: First move, Today's schedule, Goal progress, Meeting prep, To-do list, Email drafts for approval, Waiting on others, then the footer status line.
